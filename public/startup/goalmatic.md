## Problem

The customer problem, stated as an observed workflow rather than a market-size claim.

<a id="problem-core"></a>

### What problem does Goalmatic address?

_Usage: Application problem statement_

Many owner-run businesses handle enquiries, product details, appointments and follow-ups across WhatsApp, Instagram, notebooks and memory. Information gets scattered, and the owner has to hold the process together. General automation tools add another problem: they ask the owner to design a workflow before getting a useful result.

<a id="problem-why-now"></a>

### Why work on this now?

_Usage: Why-now answer without unsupported market figures_

Small businesses already meet customers through messaging and social platforms, but many still need a clear website and a simple way to organise what happens next. AI can help create the website and power useful tasks underneath. Goalmatic packages that capability into finished tools, so the owner starts with a job instead of an empty editor.

## Solution

How the website, Apps and shared account fit together.

<a id="solution-how-it-works"></a>

### How does Goalmatic work?

_Usage: General product explanation_

The owner describes the business, reviews the website Goalmatic Builder drafts, and publishes it on a free goalmatic.site address or a domain they already own. Website forms save enquiries to the owner's workspace. When another job appears, the owner adds a focused App for bookings, products or social posts. The website and Apps use the same Goalmatic account and credit balance.

<a id="solution-differentiation"></a>

### What is different about Goalmatic?

_Usage: Differentiation answer; avoid unsupported superiority claims_

Goalmatic starts with a visible outcome, a business website, then lets the owner add one focused tool at a time on the same account. It is designed around the way many owner-run businesses already work: WhatsApp, an Instagram page, a notebook and memory. This is a product choice, not a claim that Goalmatic has more features than specialist tools.

<a id="solution-website-or-app-store"></a>

### Is Goalmatic a website builder, an automation tool or an App Store?

_Usage: Use when the product category is unclear_

It is one business account. You start with the AI website builder, add Apps from the Goalmatic App Store for everyday jobs, and the AI and automation run underneath, so you do not have to build them yourself.

<a id="products"></a>

## Products

Current capabilities and the limits that should travel with every product description.

<a id="products-builder"></a>

### What does Goalmatic Builder do?

_Usage: Current Builder description_

Goalmatic Builder turns a business description into a website draft. The owner reviews and edits it before publishing on a free goalmatic.site address or a domain they already own. Forms on the published website save enquiries to the owner's Goalmatic workspace. A WhatsApp alert for each new enquiry is next to build, not available today.

<a id="products-bookins"></a>

### What does Bookins by Goalmatic do?

_Usage: Product listing or application answer with required limits_

Bookins gives a solo service provider or coach one booking link with services, prices, weekly hours and available times. Customers can choose an individual appointment without a Goalmatic account, and Bookins checks the slot again before confirming it. Bookins does not support classes, group or multi-staff booking. Payments, deposits, reminders, email confirmations, guest rescheduling, guest cancellation and calendar sync are not available yet.

<a id="products-store-studio"></a>

### What does Store Studio by Goalmatic do?

_Usage: Product listing or application answer with required limits_

Store Studio gives a product seller one public link for products, variants and prices. Customers can browse and place an order without a Goalmatic account. The owner can manage orders, stock, customers and discounts, and offer pickup or delivery. Online payments and card checkout are not available yet, so the customer arranges payment with the seller for pickup or delivery.

<a id="products-social-studio"></a>

### What does Social Studio by Goalmatic do?

_Usage: Product listing or application answer with required limits_

Social Studio helps an owner plan posts on a calendar, adjust a draft for selected connected accounts, publish now or schedule ahead, and review delivery status or errors. Publishing depends on each social network's permissions and the connected account. Some destinations require the user to finish posting on the platform, and Goalmatic cannot guarantee publishing on every network.

<a id="products-shared-account"></a>

### What do the website and Apps share?

_Usage: Use beside any one-account claim_

They use one Goalmatic sign-in, workspace and credit balance. They do not share records automatically. Each App keeps its records as Tables in the chosen workspace, asks for permission at install, and reuses an existing Table only when the owner chooses to link it.

<a id="faq"></a>

## Frequently asked questions

<a id="faq-record-sync"></a>

### Do Goalmatic Apps share my records automatically?

_Usage: Account and data FAQ_

No. Each App keeps its own records as Tables in the workspace you choose. During installation, you decide whether the App should keep separate records or use a Table you already have. The App only gets the access you approve.

<a id="faq-customer-account"></a>

### Do my customers need a Goalmatic account?

_Usage: Customer access FAQ_

No. Customers can send an enquiry through your website, choose an individual appointment through your Bookins link, or browse and order through your Store Studio link without creating a Goalmatic account.

<a id="faq-how-to-start"></a>

### How do I get started?

_Usage: Entry-point FAQ and official links_

Choose the first job. Build your website at https://goalmatic.site, or browse focused Apps at https://goalmatic.io/apps. Start with one useful result, then add another tool when you need it.

<a id="faq-whatsapp"></a>

### Can Goalmatic reply to my customers on WhatsApp?

_Usage: WhatsApp FAQ_

Not today. You can message the Goalmatic Assistant yourself by text, voice note or photo. A WhatsApp alert to the owner for each new website enquiry is next to build. AI replies to customers from the business's own WhatsApp number are planned for a later version.

<a id="faq-credits"></a>

### What are credits used for?

_Usage: Credits FAQ without plan or pricing figures_

Credits are the shared balance for AI and some metered actions across Goalmatic, the Builder and Apps. Before a metered action runs, the product should show its credit cost and ask you to confirm. Installing an App does not mean every action inside it has no cost.
